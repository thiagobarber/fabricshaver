Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What fabrics can I use NoFuzz on?

Virtually all fabric types — wool, cotton, cashmere, polyester, fleece, blends and upholstery. Choose Low for delicates, Medium for everyday garments and High for thick knits and upholstery. Spot-test a hidden area first if you're unsure.

Will it damage my clothes?

No. The rotary blade sits behind a protective safety net that lifts loose pills and cuts them at the surface, so the fabric underneath stays intact. Never press down hard — let the blade do the work.

How long does the battery last?

A full charge delivers multiple complete de-pilling sessions. Runtime depends on the speed level used, and the LCD screen shows the remaining battery percentage so you always know when to recharge.

How do I empty the lint chamber?

Twist off the chamber, empty the collected fuzz and reattach. No tools required. Clean the chamber regularly for best performance.
